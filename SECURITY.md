# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability in this tool

If you find a bug in the scanner, hooks, or defaults that could cause a
false all-clear, execute untrusted code, leak data, or otherwise weaken
the protection, please report it privately rather than opening a public
issue.

Email: adansarfaraz.dev@gmail.com

Please include the affected script, what you expected, and what happened.
Do not attach live malware samples; a reduced, inert proof is enough.

## If your machine or a repo looks infected

That is an incident on your side, not a vulnerability in this repository.
Follow the "If you are already infected" steps in the README: disconnect
the machine, rotate credentials and wallets from a different clean device,
and report it to your security team and to the platforms whose tokens may
have been stolen (GitHub, npm, and so on). SEAL recommends a full OS
reinstall for Contagious Interview infections, especially on Windows.
